Draw a workflow box by box: from **Start**, through the steps, to an **End**.

### What you do here
- Drag a box from the list on the left onto the canvas, or click it.
- Connect two boxes: drag from the dot under a box to the next box.
- Click a box or an arrow to set it up on the right.
- **Save draft** at any time; the list above the canvas says what is still missing.

### Good to know
- New here? Read the [Workflow designer guide](guide:workflow-designer/index).
- **Undo**, **Copy** and **Delete** work with Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+C and Delete (⌘ on a Mac).
- A published version stays as it is: **Edit as draft** makes a draft of it. Runs keep the published version until the draft is published.
