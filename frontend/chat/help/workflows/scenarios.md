title: Scenarios
---

A scenario adjusts how much context the agent gets for one kind of request.

### What you do here
- Tune the context for a recurring kind of request, for example summaries or presentations: skills, system context, recent history, a token budget and the tool domains it must cover.

### Good to know
- A scenario does not choose a workflow. Which workflow runs is chosen explicitly in the chat or proposed for the person to start.
