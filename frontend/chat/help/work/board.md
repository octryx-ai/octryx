title: Work items
---

A work item is the Workspace's view of a Jira issue, with its execution state next to what Jira says.

### What you do here
- Switch between My work and All items; choose once who you are in Jira.
- Filter the board, or switch to the table. Badges only mark exceptions, such as Blocked or Agent may start.
- Open an item to see its source, plan, reviewers, pull requests and history.
- Start a workflow run from the item; repository, branch and run limits are under Advanced.

### Good to know
- Source fields show what Jira says now; the others show local execution state.
- When Jira changes, the item is updated without overwriting its history.
- Keep intake filters to the team's boundary; validate a new intake with a small limit first.
