title: Plans

A plan describes the change for one work item before coding starts.

### What you do here
- Create a plan from a work item.
- Review the plans waiting for a decision.
- Open a plan to see its versions and decisions.

### Next steps
- [Open work items](/chat?area=work&work_view=board)
- [Who approves: Approval rule](/chat?area=admin&tab=approvalRule)

### A good plan
- Lists repositories, files, branch names, tests, rollback notes and risks.
- Keeps its approval decisions, so a reviewer sees why work went ahead.
- Reviewer suggestions are guidance, not approval.
