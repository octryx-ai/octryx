Work items from Jira, their plans, reviews and pull requests, with the evidence behind each step.

### What you do here
- **Work items**: the board of the Workspace's items.
- **Plans**: change plans and the ones waiting for review.
- A run's pull request is shown on the run and on its work item.
- **Intake** and **Jira write-back**: what flows in from Jira and what goes back.

### Next steps
- [Open connectors](/chat?area=admin&tab=connectors)
- [Set up intake](/chat?area=work&work_view=intake)
- [Open work items](/chat?area=work&work_view=board)

### Good to know
- Jira stays the source of business truth; Work adds plans, reviews, pull requests and audit evidence.
- Write-back to Jira is off until it is switched on.
- Who approves plans is set in the [Approval rule](/chat?area=admin&tab=approvalRule).
