title: Devices
---

Your own octryx-claude devices. A connected device can run chat requests you send to it.

### What you do here
- Connect a device with the command shown on this page.
- Pause, rename or remove a device.
- Choose the device in the chat composer's **Run on** control.

### Next steps
- [Install octryx-claude](/chat?area=agents&agents_view=install)

### Good to know
- Only you can send work to your devices or answer their approvals.
- Folders are added on the device itself.
