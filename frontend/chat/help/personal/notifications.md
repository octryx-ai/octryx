How you hear about runs and device jobs that are stuck, looping or failed.

### What you do here
- In-app alerts are always on: your runs' problems appear in the bell, the Octryx companion and as phone push.
- Turn on email, Slack or Microsoft Teams and say where alerts go.
- Send a test, change or remove an alert from the row's ⋯ menu.

### Next steps
- [Workspace alerts (admins)](/chat?area=admin&tab=channels)

### Good to know
- Email, Slack and Teams carry problems in your own runs and device jobs (you asked for the run or are responsible for it) and, if you administer a Workspace, every problem in it.
- When many problems appear at once, you get the first ones one by one and then one summary ("N more problems") that links to the Workspace's Work page.
- Each switch here covers only these alerts; your other notifications keep their own settings.
- A test is queued at once and delivered shortly after; if it does not arrive, check the address.
- On the phone, push for problems is switched under More › Notifications.
- Quiet hours are set in the Octryx companion (the gear in its panel).
