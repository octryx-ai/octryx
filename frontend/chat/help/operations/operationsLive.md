Every run going on right now: its state, where it works, its last sign of life and its cost so far.

### What you do here
- Find the run that needs you, loops or has gone silent; the most urgent come first.
- Open a team run to see its items and their device jobs.
- Answer or open a run, or abort it when it should not go on.
- Stop a job on your own device; admins acknowledge a problem until it happens again.

### Good to know
- The page refreshes every 10 seconds and pauses while the tab is hidden.
- A run waiting for a person is never counted as silent; it shows who it waits for and how long.
- *Looping* means one item was started 10 times or more in 5 minutes without moving on. *Silent* means a running run has shown no event for 20 minutes.
- Costs cover the last 24 hours of device jobs and priced usage; they are platform estimates, not provider invoices.
- Platform admins see every Workspace, Workspace admins the Workspaces they administer, members their current Workspace.
