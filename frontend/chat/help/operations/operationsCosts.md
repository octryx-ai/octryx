What the runs of a period cost and what they delivered, per workflow, work item, run, person or Workspace.

### What you do here
- Choose the period (7, 30 or 90 days) and how to group the runs.
- Compare cost with runs, delivered runs and pull requests; the most expensive come first.
- Open a run or a work item from its row.

### Good to know
- A run counts in the period it started in. *Delivered* means the run completed.
- *Cost per delivered run* is the cost of the group divided by its delivered runs; a group that delivered nothing shows a dash.
- A run's cost is its device jobs plus the priced model usage of its chats. Work on a Claude seat counts at the list price Claude Code reports.
- Costs are platform estimates, not provider invoices.
