Search finds passages in the Workspace's documents, tickets and code, each with a link to its source.

### What you do here
- Ask a question in any language.
- Narrow the results with **Filters**: by source, space or project and time.
- Open **Preview** to read a passage between its neighbours, then open it where it lives.
- Use **Ask in chat** to continue with the question and its sources in a new chat.

### Next steps
- [Open sources](/chat?area=knowledge&tab=workspaceKnowledge)
- [Open documents](/chat?area=knowledge&tab=knowledgeSources)
- [Explore relationships](/chat?area=knowledge&tab=knowledgeRelationships)

### Reading the results
- Results are grouped by the system they come from; the order within the results is the ranking.
- Passages are evidence, not reviewed conclusions: read the source before relying on them.
- Search also covers knowledge shared with this Workspace.
- The search method, products and version are under **Options**.
