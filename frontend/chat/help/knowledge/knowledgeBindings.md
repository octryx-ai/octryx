title: Bindings
---

Which other Workspaces this Workspace reads, and which Workspaces read this one. Nothing is copied: search, changes and code impact read across.

### What you do here
- Read another Workspace, for example a library with the Spring Boot packages.
- Narrow a binding to some products, and give a reason.
- Revoke a binding; either side can.

### Next steps

- [Sources](/chat?area=knowledge&tab=workspaceKnowledge)

### Rules of a binding
- You need the admin or owner role in both Workspaces.
- The source's data classification may not be above this Workspace's. A binding whose source rises above it becomes inert.
- Bindings are one-directional and not transitive.
