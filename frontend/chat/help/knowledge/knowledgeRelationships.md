title: Explore
---

Explore shows who is connected to what, and what changed, each with the source it was read from.

### What you do here
- Filter by entity or term, and by kind of relationship.
- Select an entity to see its relationships and their sources beside the list.
- Use **Search for this** to find every passage about it, or **Show in graph** to see its neighbourhood.
- Switch between **List** and **Graph**; the filter and the selected entity stay.

### Good to know
- Relationships are read from documents that carry a product and version; they are candidates for review, not verified conclusions.
- Relationships without a source are hidden until you show them.
- The graph is available to admins.
