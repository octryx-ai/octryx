Knowledge answers questions from the Workspace's sources and shows where each answer comes from.

### What you do here
- **Search** finds passages across documents, tickets and code.
- **Explore** shows what is connected to what, and what changed, each with its source.
- **Sources**, **Documents** and **Research** maintain what Knowledge learns from.

### Good to know
- Extracted passages and relationships are not reviewed conclusions; read the source before relying on them.
- Shared knowledge stays subject to Workspace and source permissions.
