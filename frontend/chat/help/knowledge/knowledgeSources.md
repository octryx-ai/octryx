The documents Knowledge searches: pages and tickets synced from the sources, and uploaded files, each with its revisions.

### What you do here
- Find a document by title or key.
- Select a row to open the document beside the list: **View** it, see its **Revisions** and the **Diff** of two.
- Open a synced document where it lives with **Open in …**, from its ⋯ menu or the open document.
- If you may edit the Workspace's knowledge, add a document by upload or remove one from the open document.

### Adding a document
- **PDF, Office and HTML** (pdf, html, docx, pptx, xlsx) up to 32 MB; **text** (txt, md, adoc, csv, json, xml) up to 8 MB. The panel shows the limits under the file field.
- After the upload the document goes through three steps:
  1. **Uploaded**: the original is stored unchanged as a new revision.
  2. **Parsed**: text files right away; PDF, Office and HTML in the isolated parser (no network), which can take a few minutes.
  3. **Indexed**: the passages become searchable and citable in this Workspace.
- Uploading a file with the same key as an existing document adds a new revision of it.

### Good to know
- The newest documents come first; load more to see older ones.
- A page deleted or moved out of the synced scope at its source is marked **Removed at source** and no longer found by Search.
- **Open as page** shows the open document full width; its address opens it again.
