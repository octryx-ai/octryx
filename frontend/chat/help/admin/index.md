Everything that is configured once for the platform and then made available to Workspaces.

### What you do here
- **Problems**: what is broken and where to fix it.
- **Workspaces**: create Workspaces and set their access.
- **Connections**: the systems Octryx AI reads from and writes to, including repositories and Jira write-back.
- **Models** and **Rules**: which models answer, and what agents may do.
- **People & sign-in**: accounts and SSO.
- **Platform**: the installed version and every person's devices.

### Good to know
- **Problems** lists operational issues; **Rules** groups policies, tool and approval rules, command safety, content security and classification.
- Credentials stay on the server and are never shown to models or users.
