title: Roles

Global roles decide what an account may do on the platform.

### What you do here
- Create a role with **+ New role**: a name and one line on what it allows.
- Open a role to change its description and permissions, or delete it.

### Next steps
- [Assign roles to users](/chat?area=admin&tab=users)
- [Check who can do what](/chat?area=admin&tab=identityAccess)

### Good to know
- The built-in admin role always has full access; other roles only grant the permissions listed on them.
- A role name uses lowercase letters, digits, hyphens and underscores.
- What someone may do inside a Workspace is set per Workspace under Access.
