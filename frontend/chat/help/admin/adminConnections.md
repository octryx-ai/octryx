title: Connections
---

Each system Octryx AI connects to is set up once here and made available to all or selected Workspaces.

### What you do here
- Add a connection: type, name, the access fields that type asks for, and where it is available. A second account is a second connection.
- Switch its uses on: **Knowledge** (what it reads, schedule or a custom cron), **Tickets** (filter, mapping to our ticket states; write-back is set in the workflow) and **Agent tools** (off, read, or read and write).
- See where a Workspace narrows it, and reset that. A Workspace admin may narrow and reset it for their own Workspace.
- Test it, read its recent errors, or delete it once nothing uses it (Delete is in the connection's ⋯ menu).
- Open **Connector settings** (page header, or a connection's ⋯ menu for its type) for the guided setup of built-in connectors, their health and tools, and private hosts for on-premises systems.
