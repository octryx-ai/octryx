title: Model catalog
---

Every model people and agents can use, from every provider, one row each.

### What you do here
- Enable a model, describe it, and clear it for data.
- Open a row to change its general settings, data clearance, generation settings and cost.
- Sync from providers to pick up new models and spot ones that are gone.

### Next steps
- [Set the platform order](/chat?area=admin&tab=modelOrder)
- [Check providers](/chat?area=admin&tab=providers)

### Good to know
- Workspaces choose only from the enabled, cleared models.
- Raising a model's clearance lets it see more sensitive data. It applies when you save and is recorded in the audit log.
