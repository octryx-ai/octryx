The data classes, and what each level may use and do.

### What you do here
- Add a level or a rule.
- Change a cell of the matrix, or look at the policy it generates.
- Submit a draft for review, then approve or reject it.

### Next steps
- [Open Policies](/chat?area=admin&tab=cedarPolicies)
- [Review content security](/chat?area=admin&tab=contentSecurity)

### Reading the matrix
- Columns are levels, from least to most sensitive. Each row is one rule; click a cell to change it or to see the generated policy.
- Only platform admins change the classification policy; everyone else can read it.
