Custom policies that block actions everywhere, on top of the generated baseline.

### What you do here
- Add a policy, or start from a template.
- Test a policy on the test bench before you publish it.
- Edit detectors that find sensitive content.
- Publish the draft and look back at the version history.

### Good to know
- Custom policies can only **block**: the generated baseline (classification matrix, Workspace policies, platform invariants) grants, and a custom policy narrows it.
- Generated rows are read-only and link to where they are edited.
- Without custom policies the generated baseline keeps applying; add a policy to block more.
