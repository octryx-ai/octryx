title: Users
---

The accounts that can sign in to the platform.

### What you do here
- Create a local account with **+ New user**: username, email, password, account type and primary role.
- Open an account to change its profile, roles, groups, password or API keys.
- Deactivate an account; its history stays and it can be reactivated later.

### Next steps
- [Set up single sign-on](/chat?area=admin&tab=identityProviders)
- [Manage roles](/chat?area=admin&tab=identityRoles)
- [Check who can do what](/chat?area=admin&tab=identityAccess)

### Good to know
- **+ New user** creates a local account with a password of at least 8 characters.
- People who sign in through SSO are created automatically on their first sign-in; that is set up under Sign-in.
- Workspace access is granted in each Workspace's settings under Access.
