order: 6
---

A draft runs nowhere. Only a published workflow can be started.

1. Click **Save draft**. The list of problems shows what is still missing; click a problem to jump to its box.
2. Fix every problem until the list is empty.
3. Click **Request publishing**. A second administrator checks the workflow and publishes it (four eyes).

**Changing a published workflow**
- A published version is never changed. Click **Edit as draft**: you get a copy to change, and the published version keeps running until the new one is published.

**Good to know**
- Runs that already started finish with the version they started with.
