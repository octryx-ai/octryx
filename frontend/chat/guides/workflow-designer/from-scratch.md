---
title: Building a workflow from scratch
order: 2
---

1. Click **New workflow** and give it a name.
2. Drag a box from the list on the left onto the canvas, or click it: it lands in a free spot and is selected.
3. Connect two boxes: drag from the dot on the right of a box to the next box. Or open the box's **⋯** menu, choose **Connect to…** and pick the box.
4. Click a box and fill in its settings on the right. Red lines say what is still missing.
5. Click **Save draft** whenever you like. A draft may be unfinished.
6. When nothing is red any more, ask for it to be published: [Publishing](guide:workflow-designer/publishing).

**Good to know**
- **Undo** and **Redo** take back or repeat your last changes.
- **Group** puts several boxes in a frame, for example "Fix"; **Ungroup** removes the frame.
- **Copy** and **Delete** work on the selected boxes.
- Every workflow needs exactly one **Start**, and every way must reach an **End**.
